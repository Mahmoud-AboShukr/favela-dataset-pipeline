# Experiments

## Purpose

This section documents the pilot experiments carried out within the favela dataset pipeline.

The project uses **Brasília** as the main experimental sandbox before scaling methods to larger regions of Brazil. These experiment pages record what was tested, why it was tested, what outputs were generated, and what conclusions were drawn.

The goal of this section is to separate:

- **stable methods** from
- **dated experimental evaluations**

so that the documentation remains clear as the project evolves.

---

## What belongs in this section

An experiment page should describe a specific evaluated workflow, for example:

- a GHSL settlement-mask pilot,
- a GHSL temporal-change study,
- a MapBiomas-only refinement pilot,
- or a later multi-source fusion comparison.

Each experiment page should explain:

- the question being asked,
- the data used,
- the main processing logic,
- the outputs produced,
- the main observations,
- and the conclusion or next step.

## Relationship to other documentation sections

- **Methods** describe reusable processing logic.
- **Experiments** describe specific pilot evaluations.
- **Status Notes** capture dated progress and day-to-day findings.
- **Decisions** record formal methodological or project choices.
- **Thesis Draft** contains research-writing material derived from methods and experiments.

This section is therefore the main place to document how candidate refinement strategies are being compared in practice.

---

## Documentation principle

When writing experiment pages, the preferred structure is:

1. objective,
2. study area and inputs,
3. method summary,
4. outputs,
5. observations,
6. limitations,
7. conclusion and next step.

This makes the experiments easier to compare and easier to reuse later in reports or thesis chapters.
